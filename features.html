Feature Tour

Built for Privacy. Built for You.

Quasar is not a browser with privacy bolted on. Every engine inside it - from the ad blocker to the network layer - exists to put you back in control of your data.

Ad Blocker

Built-in Ad Blocker

Quasar ships with its own ad blocking engine - no extensions needed. Banners, pop-ups, video pre-rolls and malicious ads are removed before they ever render, so pages load faster, use less data and expose you to far less malvertising.

🔒

What gets blocked

  • • Third-party tracking cookies and pixels
  • • Banner and pop-up ad networks
  • • Cryptominers hidden in pages
  • • Known malware and phishing domains
🕶

What is fingerprinting?

Sites combine your canvas, WebGL, fonts, screen size and timezone into a unique "fingerprint" that survives cookie deletion. Quasar randomizes these signals per session so you look like millions of other users, not the one they are hunting.

Anti-Fingerprinting

Opposes Browser Fingerprinting

Canvas, WebGL, audio context, installed fonts and window dimensions are masked and randomized every session. Tracking networks that profile you silently get a generic, unstable target instead.

Networking

Ghost Networking

Route traffic through peer relays so websites only ever see the exit node's IP address. Chain multiple hops for VPN-style anonymity without trusting any single central service.

🌏
  • • Peer relays, no central VPN to trust
  • • Exit node IP shown to sites - never yours
  • • Chain multiple hops for extra layers
  • • Per-session identity aliases
✉

Private by default

Void Mail uses an encrypted inbox built into the browser - no third-party mail server peeking at your subject lines. Void Search is the default private search inside the browser, with results served without a tracking profile.

Search + Mail

Void Search & Void Mail

A private search engine and an encrypted mail inbox live directly inside the browser. No extension, no separate app, no telemetry between the two.

Local Hosting

Vault Sites

Publish and browse entire websites that live inside your own vault folder - served straight to the browser with a custom void:// address. No web host, no server logs, no third party between you and the content.

🏠
  • • Sites hosted from your local vault
  • • void:// custom protocol addressing
  • • No server, no external logs
  • • Custom splash & start-page shortcuts
🛡

What we never do

  • • No phone-home or usage statistics
  • • No crash reports to external servers
  • • No selling or sharing of browsing data
  • • No account or signup required to browse
Trust

Zero Telemetry

There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporter phoning home, and no data about what you read or watch. Your browsing history stays on your device - nowhere else.
