Core Features

Built for Privacy

Every feature is designed with one goal: keep your browsing private, secure, and free from surveillance.

Privacy-Focused Browser

Quasar is built from the ground up with privacy as its core principle. No telemetry, no data collection, no compromises. Your browsing history stays on your device - nowhere else.

Combats Third-Party Tracking

Built-in ad blocker and tracker blocker stop third-party cookies, pixels, and scripts from following you across the web. Every request is analyzed - suspicious trackers are killed before they load.

Opposes Fingerprinting

Browser fingerprinting tracks you by your unique device characteristics. Quasar randomizes and masks fingerprint data - canvas, WebGL, fonts, screen resolution - making you blend in with millions of others.

Void Mode Encryption

Every request wrapped through four encrypted hops. Your identity is replaced with an alias at Lobby. The destination stays sealed until Void4. Uploads always ask your permission first.

Built-in Ad Blocker

No extensions needed. Quasar ships with a powerful ad blocker that removes banners, pop-ups, and malicious ads - making pages load faster and keeping you safe from malvertising.

Ghost Networking

Route traffic through peer relays so websites see the exit node's IP - not yours. Chain multiple hops for VPN-style anonymity without trusting any central service.

Security

Protection Layers

Quasar defends you on multiple fronts. Every layer is designed to keep your browsing invisible.

Anti-Tracking Engine

Real-time analysis of every network request. Third-party trackers, fingerprinting scripts, and tracking cookies are identified and blocked before they execute.

Encrypted Void Pipes

Every layer uses AEAM-style encryption - replay-proof, tamper-proof, key-separated per hop. No single node can decrypt the full path.

Fingerprint Randomization

Canvas, WebGL, audio context, fonts, and screen properties are randomized per session. Websites see a generic profile - not the real you.

No Telemetry

Zero data collection. No phone-home, no usage statistics, no crash reports sent to external servers. What you browse is known only to you.
