FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Quasar Browser really free?

Yes. Quasar is completely free to download and use. There are no paid tiers, no pro features locked away, and no account is required just to browse.

Which operating systems are supported?

Currently Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit). A portable ZIP build lets you run Quasar from a USB drive without installing anything.

Does Quasar collect any data about me?

No telemetry, no analytics SDK, no crash reporter phoning home. Your history stays in your profile on your device. Nothing about what you read or watch is ever uploaded.

What exactly is Void Mode?

Void Mode routes a request through four encrypted hops - Lobby, corepoint, Escape and Void4. Your identity is replaced with an alias at the first hop, and only the final exit hop touches the actual website. See the full Void Mode guide.

How do I set or change my home page shortcuts?

Open Quasar's Settings dialog, find the Home Page section, and add or remove shortcuts. They are stored locally in the browser profile.

Can I still use my normal bookmarks and favorites?

Yes, Quasar has a built-in favorites manager. Import and export are available from the favorites panel.

How do I update Quasar?

Download the newest installer from the download page and run it over your current install. Your profile, shortcuts and favorites are preserved.

How do I uninstall Quasar?

Use Windows Settings > Apps > Quasar Browser > Uninstall. For the portable ZIP build, just delete the folder. Nothing is left behind.

What are Vault Sites?

Vault Sites are websites you host directly from a local vault folder, served to the browser over the custom void:// protocol. There is no web host and no server log in between.

Something is broken. Where do I report it?

Check the project's public source repository for an issues section and report the problem there. Screenshots and steps to reproduce are always welcome.
